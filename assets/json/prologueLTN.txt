Long ago, the people of Lostray were incredibly smart and kind. They spent all their time learning and building beautiful things.
Because they were so nice, they never had any fights. They didn't even have a word for battle or defense in their language.
Only one man was different. He built tough, armored cars because he felt they needed protection, but the others just laughed at him
Suddenly, giant robots arrived from the stars. They were tall and scary, looking like huge statues made of dark, cold metal.
The people of Lostray didn't know how to fight back. Because they were so peaceful, the giants were able to take over the town easily.
The giants made the people work as their prisoners. They thought the humans were too calm and logical to ever try to trick them.
The giants forced the citizens to use a big machine called the Generator. They had to solve a giant puzzle made of glowing gems.
The people didn't know that this puzzle was a secret trap. It was actually a code that would turn the whole world into energy.
The giants wanted to turn everything into a snack! They planned to eat the energy from the town and leave nothing behind
The brave man drove his old armored car back into town. The giants' high-tech sensors didn't notice him because his car was so simple.
He reached the Generator and changed the glowing puzzle. He put in a noisy code that would make the machine act like a poison.
When the giants tried to take the energy, they began to shake. Their metal bodies cracked and they turned into harmless stardust.
